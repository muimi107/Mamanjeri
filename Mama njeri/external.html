History of CSS

The Proposal: In October 1994, Norwegian developer Håkon Wium Lie published the first proposal for "Cascading HTML Style Sheets" while working at CERN alongside web inventor Tim Berners-Lee. Lie introduced the concept of the "cascade," allowing multiple style sheets to contribute rules while utilizing weights to resolve conflicts.

Collaboration: Dutch programmer Bert Bos was independently developing a style sheet language for the Argo browser. The two merged their ideas, dropped "HTML" from the title to keep it language-agnostic, and officially named it Cascading Style Sheets.
